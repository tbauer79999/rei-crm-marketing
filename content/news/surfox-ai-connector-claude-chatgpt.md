SurFox AI (getsurfox.com), the AI platform that texts, chats with, and calls inbound leads, qualifies them, and books the appointment, announced that its connector is live in Claude and ChatGPT. With it, SurFox AI users can ask their AI assistant about their leads and get answers straight from their SurFox AI account, without logging in to a separate dashboard. The connector is listed in both Claude's connector directory and ChatGPT's app directory.

## What users can ask

Through the connector, a SurFox AI user can:

- Find a lead by phone number or email
- List the leads marked hot in a time window, such as the last 7 days
- Read the full message history with a lead
- List their active campaigns
- Check this cycle's text usage against their plan limit

A user can ask "Using SurFox, how many hot leads do I have in the last 7 days?" or "What lead is attached to this phone number?" and get the answer in the same conversation.

## Read-only by design

The connector only reads data. It cannot send a text, launch or pause a campaign, create or change a lead, or see billing or payment details. Conversations with leads stay with SurFox AI, which runs the talk track the business approved. The assistant helps the owner see what is happening.

## What SurFox AI does

SurFox AI responds to inbound leads by SMS, website chat, and Voice AI. It qualifies each lead, handles objections, and books the appointment. All three channels share the same talk track and the same learning.

SurFox AI builds each business's talk track, qualifying questions, and objection handling, and every talk track is 100% customized to the business it represents. Businesses can run different campaigns for different angles, each with automatic follow-up. On the Growth plan and up, SurFox AI learns which messages actually lead to bookings and leans into them. When a lead is ready to book, SurFox AI checks the business's Google or Outlook calendar for availability, books the slot, and emails an .ics calendar invite.

## Founder perspective

"Business owners are already running their day from Claude and ChatGPT, so that's where they should be able to check on their leads," said Tom Bauer, founder and CEO of SurFox AI. "Ask how many hot leads came in this week and you get the answer right there. We built it read-only on purpose, so your assistant can look but never text a lead or change a campaign."

## How to get started

SurFox AI customers can find SurFox AI in Claude's connector directory or ChatGPT's app directory, sign in with their SurFox AI account, and approve access. Businesses new to SurFox AI can request a login to a live demo account. It is exactly what SurFox AI gives its customers: no sales pitch, just the product. More information is available at getsurfox.com.
