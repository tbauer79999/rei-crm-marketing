SurFox AI (getsurfox.com), the AI platform that texts, chats with, and calls inbound leads, launched Voice AI. SurFox AI now answers inbound lead calls, qualifies the caller, handles objections, and books the appointment while the caller is still on the line. Voice AI joins SurFox AI's existing SMS and website chat channels, so a business can respond to a new lead by text, chat, or phone from one platform.

The launch comes as AI assistants start contacting businesses on their users' behalf. Google's AI has called local businesses for searchers since 2025 to check pricing and availability, and in May 2026 Google announced it was expanding that calling to categories including home repair, beauty, and pet care, in addition to auto repair. Meta has also begun testing assistant-placed calls to businesses.

For a business, the practical result is that a missed call can now mean a lost booking. An assistant working through a list on someone's behalf has little reason to wait for a callback.

## How SurFox AI Voice AI works

SurFox AI builds each business's talk track, qualifying questions, and objection handling. Every talk track is 100% customized to the business it represents. The same talk track runs across SMS, website chat, and Voice AI, and all three channels share the same learning.

When a caller is ready to book, SurFox AI checks the business's Google or Outlook calendar for open times, books the slot, and emails the lead an .ics calendar invite.

Businesses can run different campaigns for different angles, each with automatic follow-up. On the Growth plan and up, SurFox AI learns which messages actually lead to bookings and leans into them.

## Founder perspective

"Every missed call is a booking that goes to somebody else, and now some of those calls come from AI assistants that won't wait for a callback," said Tom Bauer, founder and CEO of SurFox AI. "Voice AI picks up on the first ring, qualifies the caller, and books the appointment on the spot, with the same talk track SurFox AI already runs for that business by text and chat."
